Final(ish) is a free, open-source Frame.io client for Android phones and tablets. Frame.io has no Android app of its own, so Final(ish) brings the Frame.io video review workflow to Android: editors, producers and clients can review cuts, leave timecoded comments and approve versions on the go.

REVIEW
• Frame-accurate player with timecode, 0.25–2× speed, loop, real resolutions up to 4K and picture-in-picture
• Timecoded and range comments, drawings on the frame, emoji, mark as done
• Aspect-ratio masks (2.39, 1.85, 9:16…) and title/action safe guides
• Compare two versions side by side or with a wipe slider
• Images, PDFs, audio and Office documents

APPROVE AND SHARE
• Change status, rating and custom metadata fields
• Create share links and edit every setting: passphrase, expiry, downloads, watermark
• Open Frame.io links straight in the app

UPLOAD AND DOWNLOAD
• Upload clips, photos and whole folders from your phone, in the background, resuming after a network drop
• Download files and folders, or keep copies offline for review without a connection

AND MORE
• Notifications for new comments, mentions and opened share links, with no server needed
• Account-wide search
• Works on every Frame.io plan, Free included
• Phones and tablets, portrait and landscape
• English, Italian, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil)
• Demo mode: try the app without an account

Sign in with your own Adobe ID; you need an existing Frame.io account (V4).

To open Frame.io links in the app, turn on Frame.io links in the app's Settings › Permissions.
