150 words

Final(ish) is a free, open-source, unofficial Android app for Frame.io, made by Matteo Maraffio, an independent developer in Italy. Frame.io has no official Android app (as of November 2026), so Final(ish) brings the review workflow to Android phones and tablets. Editors, producers and clients can browse projects, play videos frame by frame with timecode, leave time-coded and range comments with drawings, compare two versions side by side or with a wipe, change status and ratings, and create or edit share links. Uploads run in the background and resume after a network drop, and files can be kept offline. The app uses the official Frame.io V4 API, signs in with an Adobe ID and works on every Frame.io plan, Free included. It is on Google Play in six languages, with no ads and no tracking, under the GPL-3.0 licence. It is not affiliated with Adobe or Frame.io.

Key facts

  • Frame.io has no official Android app, as of November 2026 (source: Frame.io Help Center, “Android App?”).
  • Final(ish) uses the official Frame.io V4 API, and users sign in with their own Adobe ID.
  • It works on every Frame.io plan, Free included; features a plan lacks are shown greyed out.

Key features

  • Frame-accurate video player with timecode, 0.25–2× speed, loop, resolutions up to 4K, aspect-ratio masks and picture-in-picture
  • Time-coded and range comments, drawings on the frame, mark as done
  • Compare two versions side by side or with a wipe
  • Status, rating and custom metadata fields for approvals
  • Create and edit share links: passphrase, expiry, downloads, watermark
  • Background uploads that resume after a network drop; offline files and comments
  • Images, PDFs, audio and Office documents
  • Notifications with no server, six languages, demo mode with no account

How to describe it

Final(ish) is an independent project. Please describe it as a third-party app that works with Frame.io, never as a Frame.io or Adobe product.

Do write

  • “Final(ish), an unofficial Android app for Frame.io”
  • “Final(ish) works with Frame.io”
  • “a free, open-source Android app that works with Frame.io”
  • “an independent developer”, “third-party app”

Don’t write

  • “the Frame.io Android app” or “Frame.io for Android”
  • “Frame.io’s Android app” or “Adobe’s new app”
  • “official”, “approved by Adobe” or “made with Adobe”
  • Don’t show Adobe or Frame.io logos or icons next to the app

Known limitations

Personal Adobe IDs can sign in; company or school Adobe accounts (Enterprise or Federated ID) may not be able to sign in yet, until Adobe reviews the app or their admin allows third-party apps. Legacy (V3) Frame.io accounts are not supported. Members with the Viewer role see files on Frame.io in the browser, because Frame.io gives apps no media for them. The public API does not yet allow replying to comments or creating @mentions: replies are shown, and “Reply” opens the file on Frame.io.
